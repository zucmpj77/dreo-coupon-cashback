## Rewards points: a discount with expiry dates on both ends

Dreo's points program is a third discount layer, and it has more fine print than either of the first two. Signing up for a store account gets you **500 points** to start. Earning runs at **1 point per $1 spent**, boosted to 5 points per $1 during designated promotion windows. Points convert to checkout discounts with a **maximum of 20% off per order**, subject to minimum point thresholds, and Dreo's rewards page advertises redemptions of up to $160 off at the top tiers.

The expiry rules are where people get burned:

- **Points expire 365 days after they're issued**
